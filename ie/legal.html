Privacy policy

guitle doesn't ask for any personal data to use the site: no sign-up, no newsletters, no analytics or advertising tools that follow you. We only count visits anonymously and, if you ask for a price alert, we keep your email address for that alert.

  • Price alerts. If you ask us to tell you when a product's price drops, we store your email address, the product, its price that day and your target price if you give one. We use them only to send the confirmation email and the alerts for that product; never for marketing, and we never share them. Legal basis: your consent, given when you request the alert and confirm your email. They're stored in Google Cloud Firestore (Google Ireland Ltd., server in Madrid) and emails are sent from our mailbox hola@guitle.com (DonDominio, Spain). Unconfirmed alerts are deleted after 7 days; confirmed ones are kept until you unsubscribe with the link in every email, which deletes it straight away, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Preferences in your browser. Filters or the view you choose are saved in your own browser's local storage (localStorage). They're never sent to any server.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub may log each visitor's IP address for technical and security reasons. GitHub participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Visit counter. Each page adds 1 to an anonymous counter: the day, the page viewed, the site you came from (for example google.com), what you type into the catalogue search and, if you click through to a shop, the shop's name. No cookies, no IP address, nothing that identifies you. The counters are stored in Google Cloud Firestore (Google Ireland Ltd.), on a server in the European Union (Madrid).

  • Going to a shop. When you click through to a shop you leave guitle, and that shop's privacy policy applies.
